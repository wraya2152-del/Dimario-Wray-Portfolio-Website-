// 03 — Process

How friction becomes a tool.

  • Step 01

    Spot the friction

    Sit inside the workflow and find the task that quietly eats hours every week.

  • Step 02

    Design the fix

    Decide what the tool should do and, just as importantly, what it shouldn’t.

  • Step 03

    Build it

    Turn the design into a working web tool that runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

  • Step 04

    Test it on real work

    Put it in front of the people who use it and watch where it still hurts.

  • Step 05

    Refine, then repeat

    Smooth the rough edges, then go looking for the next friction.

// 04 — Method
